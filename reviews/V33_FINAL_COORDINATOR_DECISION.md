## Resource and failure limitations

Actual planning CPU costs are included in labels. They are measured process costs, not a controlled latency benchmark. Historical orchestration included mistaken duplicate starts and process termination by the coordinator; some retry logs/progress were overwritten. These failures must not be described as exclusively natural simulator failures. Final committed shards do not restore missing historical resource receipts. Total interrupted CPU use and true peak memory remain UNKNOWN where not recorded. Per-shard time sums are not end-to-end wall time. No universal end-to-end real-time advantage is established.

## Research conclusion

The current frozen scenario/replanner provides too few positive ordinary gains to justify learning a gain predictor. Preserve this negative result. Existing-ET may remain a comparison baseline, but has not been shown superior or safe by this experiment. Further research should first resolve safety-verification mismatches and candidate/opportunity coverage in a newly specified experiment; this completed run must remain unchanged.

Public archiving is a reproducibility delivery, not a successful scientific milestone. No success tag or ENGINEERING_PASS is warranted. The fresh audit verdict is WARN; read its exact evidence scope and hashes in EXPERIMENT_AUDIT.md/json.
